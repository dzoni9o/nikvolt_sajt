Voždovac is a municipality where within a few kilometres you go from dense blocks to individual houses, and the installations follow.

## Blocks — Autokomanda, Dušanovac, Konjarnik, Medaković

Stock from the seventies and eighties. The typical finding is **a board that has already been reworked once**, but only partly: an RCD was added to some circuits and the rest left on the old logic. That is worse than either clean option, because nobody knows what is protected and what is not.

The other frequent case is **breakers tripping when two larger appliances run together**. In flats where air conditioning was later added to an existing circuit, that is not a fault — it is sizing.

In some buildings at Medaković and Konjarnik the problem comes from the shared supply; measurement shows a voltage drop that does not originate in the flat. The report then goes to the building manager, because it is not a job that gets solved inside the flat.

## Houses — Banjica, Kumodraž, Jajinci

Individual builds, mostly seventies and eighties, with two recurring findings:

**An electrode that has never been measured.** In a house, earthing is the only protection against a fault reaching an appliance body, and a tape put in the ground in that period is rarely still in good contact.

**Outdoor wiring added to over the years** — garden lighting, a garage feed, a connection for a well pump. Moisture in those sections is the most common cause of seasonal RCD tripping: it appears after rain and disappears once things dry out.

## What we do most

In the blocks, [replacing and adding protection](/usluge/fuse-replacement) and diagnosing tripping; in the houses, earthing checks and putting outdoor wiring right.

## Response time

Autokomanda, Dušanovac and Konjarnik **45 to 75 minutes**. Kumodraž and Jajinci are at the upper end.
