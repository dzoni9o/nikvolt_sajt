In Stari grad the wiring is the oldest in the city and access the most constrained. Together that means more measuring and planning here than chasing.

## What we find

**Wiring from several periods in the same flat.** The original from when it was built, a replacement from the sixties, a partial one from the nineties, and something recent. The layers are invisible until something is opened, so nothing here gets estimated from the look of the board.

**Cloth insulation.** On the oldest sections the insulation crumbles at a touch. That is the one finding where we do not plan in stages, only agree a date.

**Aluminium and copper on the same joint**, without a proper connector. Corrosion there is a slow process ending in overheating, and it is the most common cause of scorched outlets in this part of town.

**Junction boxes buried or painted over.** Finding a fault can therefore take longer than fixing it; that is why on larger jobs we return every box to an accessible position and photograph the routes before closing up.

## Constraints we respect

In listed buildings and with original joinery we often use **surface conduit following existing lines** rather than chasing. It is not prettier, but it is the only approach that leaves alone what must be left alone.

## What we do most

[Diagnostics](/usluge/fault-diagnostics) — because here the cause is rarely visible without measurement — replacing scorched outlets, and reworking the board as the first stage of larger work.

## Response time

Dorćol, Skadarlija, Kosančićev venac, Terazije — usually **30 to 60 minutes**. The very centre can be slower because of parking rather than distance.
