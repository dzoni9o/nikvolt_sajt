Savski venac has the oldest and most valuable individual housing in Belgrade, and that changes both technique and constraints.

## What we find

**Interwar villas with wiring in layers.** The original from the thirties, a replacement from the sixties, a partial one from the nineties. Under one faceplate you find copper and aluminium, cloth and PVC, on the same joint — a combination that corrodes and ends in overheating.

**Larger installations than a flat.** A house with a garden means more circuits, outdoor lighting, a garage feed, sometimes a pool or a generator. A [new installation](/usluge/new-installation) here is more often a whole project than an intervention.

**An electrode from the year of construction.** In buildings this old the tape in the ground lost its contact long ago. [Measuring earth resistance](/usluge/earthing) is the first thing we do, because in a house that is the only protection between a fault on an appliance body and a person.

## Constraints we respect

In listed buildings and around original joinery we do not chase. We run **surface conduit in trunking** that follows the existing lines. Not prettier, but the only approach that leaves alone what must be left alone.

The same goes for lighting: [fitting to old ceilings](/usluge/lighting) needs a load check before anything is hung, and on decorative ceilings it is not always possible exactly where you would want it.

## Smart systems without breaking walls

Because chasing is constrained, [smart modules](/usluge/smart-home) pay off particularly well here — they sit behind an existing switch and work with the wiring already there.

Dedinje, Senjak, Topčidersko brdo, Prokop — usually **30 to 60 minutes**.
