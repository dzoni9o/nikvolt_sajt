Zemun is two different stories in one municipality, and that changes the approach.

## The old core

In the houses and buildings of the old part, the installation has usually been **extended in several stages** — something from the sixties, something from the nineties, something last year. Junction boxes are buried or painted over, and nobody knows which route goes where.

Nothing here gets estimated by eye. We measure insulation and continuity per circuit and produce a survey before quoting, because the difference between "replace the board" and "replace the board and then discover half the wiring is finished" is a whole order of magnitude.

Walls are often thick solid brick, which changes both the cost of chasing and whether a cable can be pulled through without breaking the wall open.

## The houses out towards Batajnica

Zemun Polje, Altina, Galenika, Batajnica — mostly houses, often with **an electrode that has never been measured**. In a house, earthing is not a formality: it is the only protection standing between a fault on an appliance body and a person, and a tape put in the ground in the eighties is rarely still making the contact it once did.

The second common finding is outdoor wiring — garden lighting, a feed to the garage, a socket in the shed — run without proper moisture protection. That is the classic cause of an RCD that trips after rain.

## What we do most

[Earthing inspection and repair](/usluge/earthing) in the houses, board reworks in the old-core flats, and diagnostics where the installation has been altered repeatedly.

## Response time

Central Zemun and Gardoš **60 to 90 minutes**. Batajnica and Zemun Polje are at the upper end and occasionally beyond — if it is urgent, say so at the start so we can plan the order.
