Zvezdara has the sharpest contrast between two kinds of building of any municipality we cover.

## Mirijevo — self-built

This finding is specific to Zvezdara. Much of Mirijevo went up between the seventies and the nineties, often **in stages and without a design** — floor by floor, as money allowed.

The effect on the wiring is characteristic:

- distribution was added as it went, so it follows no circuit logic
- junction boxes are buried or cannot be found
- one house holds sections from three periods and two materials
- earthing is often done "so that it exists", without measurement

Nothing is estimated by eye here. [Insulation and continuity measurement per circuit](/usluge/fault-diagnostics) plus a survey is the first visit, and only after it does talking about price make sense.

## Vukov spomenik, Cvetkova pijaca

Older, denser stock, closer to the Vračar profile: buildings altered in layers across decades. A frequent finding is the neutral broken instead of the line — the lampholder stays live with the light switched off. We correct it whenever we find it, regardless of what we were called for.

## Konjarnik and Mokri Lug

Mixed: eighties blocks plus individual builds. In the houses the usual job is [an earthing check](/usluge/earthing), in the flats a [board rework](/usluge/consumer-units).

## Response time

Vukov spomenik, Konjarnik and Cvetkova pijaca **40 to 70 minutes**. Mirijevo and Mokri Lug at the upper end.
