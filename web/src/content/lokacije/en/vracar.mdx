Vračar has the oldest housing stock of the municipalities we cover, and that determines almost everything.

## What we find

**Installations that have outlived several renovations.** In interwar buildings the original wiring was replaced long ago, but in layers — each owner added something. So under one faceplate you find copper and aluminium, cloth and PVC insulation, all in the same place.

**The neutral broken instead of the line.** This turns up routinely in flats of this period. It means the lampholder is live with the switch off — not a hazard you can see, but a hazard. We correct it whenever we find it, regardless of what we were called for.

**Bathrooms with no protective conductor.** The original design never had one there. This is the one item in Vračar we recommend fixing without hesitation, even when nothing is currently going wrong.

**Thick solid-brick walls.** That changes both cost and technique: chasing is slower, but there is room for surface conduit, which in listed buildings is often the only option.

## What we do most

Outlet replacement with a look at what sits behind them (in Vračar, "behind" is usually more interesting than the socket itself), lighting installation, and full rewires during renovations.

If you are renovating, get in touch **before the builders start** — a [new installation](/usluge/new-installation) is planned while the walls are bare.
