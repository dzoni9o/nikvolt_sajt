Čukarica reads as two municipalities: planned blocks, and the older individual housing around them.

## Cerak, Julino brdo, Petlovo brdo

Cerak Vinogradi went up in the seventies to a single design, which works in our favour — flats within a block are almost identical, so a finding in one repeats next door.

Typically we find a board with no residual current device, and distribution sized for a household that had neither air conditioning nor an induction hob. Add both to an existing circuit and the breaker trips — that is not a fault, it is the consequence of a calculation made half a century ago.

The answer is almost always a [consumer unit rework](/usluge/consumer-units) with the circuits split, not a bigger breaker.

## Banovo brdo and Žarkovo

Older, mixed stock: sixties and seventies blocks plus a lot of houses. In the houses the most common finding is [an earth electrode that has never been measured](/usluge/earthing) — tape put in the ground in that period rarely still makes the contact it once did.

The second is outdoor wiring added to over the years: garden lighting, a garage feed, a socket for tools. Moisture there is the classic cause of [an RCD that trips after rain](/blog/why-rcd-trips).

## Industrial legacy along the Sava

Part of Čukarica by the river has buildings converted from former plants into units and warehouses. Distribution there is often three-phase and sized for machines long gone, so moving in usually means [adapting it to the new use](/usluge/industrial-installations) rather than a rewire.

## Response time

Banovo brdo, Cerak and Žarkovo usually **45 to 75 minutes**. Železnik at the upper end.
