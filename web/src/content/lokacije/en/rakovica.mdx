Rakovica is mostly blocks, built on slopes, with an industrial legacy at the foot.

## Miljakovac, Kanarevo brdo, Petlovo brdo, Labudovo brdo

Seventies and eighties stock, standard designs. As elsewhere from that period the boards have no RCD, and the kitchen circuit was built for a cooker and little else.

What is specific here is **the ground**. The buildings sit on slopes, so ground floors and cellars are partly buried and noticeably damper than the rest. Junction boxes and boards in those parts age faster than the same fittings two floors up — insulation gives gradually, and it only shows in measurement.

That is why work here often starts with [measurement per circuit](/usluge/fault-diagnostics) before any job is agreed.

## The shared riser

In some buildings the problem does not come from the flat. When measurement shows a voltage drop that the flat's load cannot explain, the cause is the shared run above the meters. The report then goes to the building manager — that is not a job solved behind the flat door, and it is fair to say so at once.

## Industrial legacy

The foot of Rakovica has buildings converted from former plants. Three-phase distribution there is sized for machines long gone, so moving into a unit usually means [adapting the distribution to the new use](/usluge/industrial-installations), with the load balanced across phases.

## Response time

Miljakovac, Kanarevo brdo and Petlovo brdo **45 to 75 minutes**. Resnik and Kijevo at the upper end.
