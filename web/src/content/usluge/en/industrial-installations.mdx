In a commercial space a fault costs more than the repair — it costs the day you could not trade. So the emphasis is different from a flat: reliability first, everything else after.

## What we do

**Three-phase distribution** — sized to actual load and balanced across phases. Unevenly loaded phases are the most common hidden problem in commercial units and the easiest to fix once measured.

**Control panels** — assembly, wiring and labelling, with a drawing that stays in the panel.

**Motors and machines** — connection, protection, motor starters and thermal overloads set to actual current rather than to whatever was there.

**Adapting a space to a new use** — the most common job when taking on a unit whose distribution was built for something else entirely.

## What always goes with the job

Measurements: insulation, loop impedance, earth electrode resistance, phase sequence. In a commercial space that is not a formality — it is what you have to show at an inspection and to an insurer.

A drawing and labelling. If the next fault turns up on a Sunday evening, the difference between a labelled and an unlabelled panel is several hours.
