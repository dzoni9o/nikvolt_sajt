An EV charger is the only load in a home that draws full current for hours without a break. That is why it does not go on an existing socket: it gets its own circuit and its own protection.

## What the installation covers

**A survey before the quote.** Meter, contracted power, consumer unit and the cable route to the charging spot. That tells us whether it is a single-phase or three-phase charger, and how powerful.

**A dedicated circuit from the consumer unit.** Nothing else on it, with the [cable size](/pojmovnik/cable-size) worked out from current and run length. For garages and driveways, length is what moves the cable size most.

**Protection in the consumer unit.** A breaker for that circuit and an [RCD](/pojmovnik/rcd) of the right type. A charger can produce DC leakage current that an ordinary RCD does not see, so it takes a type B, or a type A if the charger has built-in DC detection. The charger manual states this, and we do not improvise on it.

**[Earthing](/usluge/earthing), measured.** A charger will not run without sound earthing, and it should not.

**Mounting and commissioning.** Charger on the wall or a post, maximum current set, a test charge with your car.

## Single-phase or three-phase

A single-phase charger gives about 3.7 kW, which covers most daily driving overnight. A three-phase charger goes up to 11 kW but needs a [three-phase supply](/pojmovnik/single-and-three-phase). If you already have one, it is usually the better choice because the load is spread across three phases.

## When the house has no headroom for another big load

Better chargers have load management: they watch total consumption and cut charging back on their own when the water heater, cooker or air conditioning switches on. The car charges a little slower and the main breaker stays in. That is often cheaper and quicker than raising your contracted power.

## What to send us

A photo of the consumer unit, a photo of the meter and where you want the charger, with a rough distance from the consumer unit. If you already have a charger or a model in mind, tell us which. From that we tell you what is needed and what it costs.
