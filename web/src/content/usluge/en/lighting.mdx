Lighting is the only part of an installation you look at every day, which is why mistakes in it are the ones that get noticed.

## What we do

**Pendants and ceiling fittings** — with a load check on the ceiling, which is mandatory in plasterboard and frequently a surprise in the old ceilings of Stari grad and Vračar.

**Downlights** — positions set out before drilling, a check on what sits above the ceiling (you do not drill blind where the installation runs), and transformers left somewhere you can reach.

**Dimmers** — with a compatibility check against the lamps you will actually use.

**Outdoor lighting** — terrace, garden, entrance. Moisture protection is what matters here: outdoor circuits are the most common cause of seasonal RCD tripping.

## What we check before hanging anything

Whether the switch breaks **the line and not the neutral**. This turns up routinely in older flats and is the clearest sign the installation needs a wider look — a lampholder that stays live with the light off is a hazard that does not look like one.

Whether the lighting circuit has an earth if the fitting has a metal body.

Whether the circuit is overloaded — with LEDs that is rarely a problem, but old halogen circuits were often sized right at the limit.
