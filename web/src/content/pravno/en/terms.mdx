## What a photo-based estimate is

When you send photos and a description of the fault, you get an **indicative estimate** — the likely cause, a risk level, a price range and a proposed time for the visit.

That estimate is informational. It is made from what the photos show, and a photo does not show the state of the cable inside the wall, insulation resistance, the quality of joints in the junction boxes, or how loaded the circuit is. The final price and the final diagnosis are established **only by an on-site inspection**.

If the job turns out on site to be different from what the photo suggested, we say so before starting and give a new quote. We never begin work whose price has not been accepted first.

## What the estimate is not

- **Not a test report or certificate.** Certifying an installation means measurements with a written record, which is a separate service.
- **Not a guaranteed price.** A range from an estimate is a range, not a fixed quote.
- **Not DIY instructions.** If the estimate says something is dangerous, that means "do not touch it", not "here is how to fix it".

## Appointments and cancellation

Appointments are agreed by phone after the estimate. If you cannot make the agreed time, call the number on the site — we do not charge for cancellations.

If we travel to the agreed address and nobody answers, the call-out is charged at the listed rate.

## Prices

Prices shown on the site are **indicative and exclude materials**, except where explicitly stated otherwise. The call-out fee is deducted from the repair price if the work is carried out during that same visit.

Prices may change; the quote you receive before work starts is what counts, not the figure on the site.
